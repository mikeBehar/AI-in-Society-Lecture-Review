# AI in Society - Lecture Reviews

This repository contains HTML pages with comprehensive summaries of lectures exploring the impact of artificial intelligence on society.

## Lectures

1. **Introduction to AI Ethics** - Foundations of ethical AI development and deployment
2. **AI and Employment** - The transformation of work in the age of artificial intelligence
3. **Bias in AI Systems** - Understanding and addressing algorithmic discrimination
4. **Privacy and Surveillance** - AI, data collection, and the future of personal privacy

## How to View

To view the lecture summaries:

1. Clone this repository
2. Open `index.html` in your web browser
3. Navigate between lectures using the navigation menu or lecture cards

Alternatively, you can view the files directly by opening any HTML file:
- `index.html` - Main landing page
- `lecture1.html` - Introduction to AI Ethics
- `lecture2.html` - AI and Employment
- `lecture3.html` - Bias in AI Systems
- `lecture4.html` - Privacy and Surveillance

## Features

- Clean, responsive design that works on desktop and mobile devices
- Easy navigation between lectures
- Comprehensive summaries with key points and discussion topics
- Professional styling with CSS

## About

These lecture reviews provide insights into critical questions about how AI is shaping our world, from ethical considerations to practical implications for employment, privacy, and social justice.
